About Mickey's Wood Shop

Located in Houston, Texas near the 610 Loop and South Post Oak area, Mickey's Wood Shop specializes in custom furniture, cabinetry, built-ins, workshop solutions, fine woodworking, outdoor projects, and personalized commissions.


Every project is designed around the customer's needs and built with care, quality materials, and attention to detail.
